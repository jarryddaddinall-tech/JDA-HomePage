About

Clear Year is a personal wellness and habit-tracking tool. It helps you track alcohol-free days, reflect in a journal, and celebrate milestones along the way. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment — just a calm space to show up for yourself.

Everything stays on your device. No sign-up required for core features, no ads, and optional notifications for gentle check-ins. A home-screen widget keeps your streak visible without opening the app.
